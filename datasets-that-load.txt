HISTORY / PREVIOUS FAILURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Before the fix, 5 series failed to load:
    manifest-1787681973389_DICOM/series_4
    manifest-1788373825860_DICOM/series_1
    manifest-1788373825860_DICOM/series_2
    manifest-1788373825860_DICOM/series_6
    manifest-1788373825860_DICOM/series_7

They all threw "Caught exception: No valid DICOM slices loaded".

The cause was the DICOM transfer syntax used by those datasets:

    Transfer Syntax UID : 1.2.840.10008.1.2
    Name                : Implicit VR Little Endian

The loader only handled Explicit VR Little Endian (1.2.840.10008.1.2.1).
It read a 2-byte VR field after every tag that does not exist in implicit
VR, lost alignment, and never reached the pixel-data tag (7FE0,0010).

Fixed in src/app/dicom_loader.cpp by detecting the transfer syntax from
tag (0002,0010) in the File Meta Group and, for Implicit VR Little
Endian, parsing each element with a 32-bit length and no VR field.
See ModificationsMade/ImplicitVRSupport.md for details.
